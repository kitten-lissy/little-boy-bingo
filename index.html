The event

  • Dates: started 8 pm EST Thu 19 Nov, runs to 20 Dec. All event times are EST.
  • Scoring: every tile is 1 point. If Little Boys and Big Boys finish the same square on their boards, that square scores double (×2).
  • ☆ tiles: drops from the other board count when you do the trip together.
  • Proof: screenshot the drop with the chat box open and the event password typed (Clan Events plugin), and post it in the team screenshots chat.
  • Prizes (Little Boys): 1st 10m each, 2nd 5m. (Big Boys: 1st 60m, 2nd 30m.)

How to use this page

  • Little Boy board and Big Boy board: every tile with ✅ done, 🟡 who's on it, or ⚪ nobody yet. ×2 marks squares where finishing would score double.
  • Players: pick anyone to see what they're going for, what they can and can't do, and what's new since the start.
  • Updates by itself every hour from WikiSync, TempleOSRS and the hiscores. Tiles the tracker can't see are marked by hand.

How to sync your collection log

Open your collection log every so often so the tracker sees new drops. Install WikiSync in RuneLite, log in, and open your collection log once. Or install TempleOSRS, search your name on templeosrs.com and click Update, then press the Temple button in your collection log.
